# High-Conversion Executive SCM Outreach Playbook

This playbook provides targeted, high-conversion communication templates designed for a Washington University in St. Louis (WashU) Supply Chain Management graduate to directly reach Senior Managers, Directors, and VPs of Supply Chain, Logistics, Procurement, and S&OP.

---

## The Core Philosophy: "Give Value First"
99% of job seekers send:
> *"Hi, I graduated from XYZ with an SCM degree. Are you hiring for any entry-level roles?"*

Hiring managers ignore these because it creates work for them.
Instead, your message opens with:
> *"I noticed your DSI increased by 4 days in your recent 10-K. I built an interactive stochastic model specifically analyzing how supplier lead-time variance impacts your balance sheet, and I recorded a 90-second video walkthrough showing how $97M in Free Cash Flow could be unlocked without hurting OTIF."*
